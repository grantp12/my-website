I started a new job! Yes, like the title says, I have started a new position with a new company. I will now be working with Darktrace as a Sales Development Representative, marking a new journey in my career within the cybersecurity space.

I'm so excited about what this means for my career, as it directly fits into my interests and career goals. I want to be working with technical products and technical stakeholders, and I want to be working to influence outcomes and grow business.

Additionally, I look at this as a personal growth challenge. Sales is hard. You have to deal with rejection and keep up constant call activity and outreach. Cross-functional work and collaboration are crucial, and none of the day-to-day work you put in matters if it does not lead to you hitting your goals.

Hitting goals and overcoming obstacles at work or in sales is something that can be directly related to any challenges or issues you go through in your personal life. I'm not going to give any corny metaphor or example from my personal life to try and connect the two, but I think you get the gist. If you stay persistent and use strategies and frameworks in your work life to meet goals, you can most definitely do the same in your personal life to meet your personal goals and overcome challenges.

Another avenue of personal growth I look at is being more sociable and outgoing, which, candidly, I admit I have not been in the past. In sales, you quite literally have no choice but to embody being outgoing and work on how to connect with others. I don't think I need to go into detail about how that can help in personal relationships, friendships, communities, whatever.

Anyway, enough about me. I made this blog post to share my initial thoughts on stepping into this role, and I think it will be a nice milestone or place to check in on. Six months to a year from now, I can look back at this blog and think about how much I've grown (hopefully) since starting.

Here's to new avenues!
